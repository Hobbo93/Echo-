# Brand Studio: start a clothing brand with no stock and no upfront cost

Echo's **Brand Studio** comes up with designs, makes mock-up pictures, writes product listings and plans your launch. **Print-on-demand** does the printing: when someone buys, the printer makes that one item and posts it to them. You hold no stock.

## How the money works
- A customer pays your price, e.g. **$45** for a tee.
- The print company charges you its base cost for that one shirt plus postage, e.g. about **$20–25**. This only happens when you've made a sale.
- You keep the difference. Nothing is charged until something sells.

## One-time setup (free, about 15 minutes)
1. Make a free account at **printify.com** (or printful.com).
2. In Shopify: **Apps** → search **Printify** → **Install**, then connect it to your store.
3. In Printify, pick a product (e.g. a Gildan tee), upload your design file (PNG, 4500×5400 px, transparent background) and set your price. Make the print file in **Canva** (free) or Printify's own design tool.
4. Publish it to Shopify. It appears in your store.

## Using Brand Studio (Telegram)
- `my brand is Stringman, streetwear for Aussie gamers` sets your brand. Echo also starts researching clothing trends.
- `design ideas` gives 5 original designs that fit your brand.
- `mockup 2` makes a free picture of idea 2, good for testing interest on TikTok or Instagram before you print anything.
- `listing 2` writes the title, description, tags and a suggested price.
- `add design 2 to my store` puts it in Shopify as a **draft** (hidden) with the picture, after your Yes.
- `launch plan` gives a 4-week plan using your free channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).

## Good to know
- Designs must be original: no brand logos, cartoon characters or other people's slogans.
- AI mock-ups are rough previews. Make the real print file in Canva or Printify.
- Order a sample of each product for yourself before promoting it.
